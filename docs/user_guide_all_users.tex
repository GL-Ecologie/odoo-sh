\hypertarget{introduction}{%
\subsection{1. Introduction}\label{introduction}}

This guide covers the day-to-day use of the GL-Ecologie planning system
implemented using the Odoo platform. \textbf{Only non-manager related
information is described in this document}. Manager-related
documentation will follow at a future time.

If you are new to the system, start with §2 (Getting Started) and then
follow the track that matches your role.

\begin{center}\rule{0.5\linewidth}{0.5pt}\end{center}

\hypertarget{getting-started}{%
\subsection{2. Getting Started}\label{getting-started}}

\hypertarget{accessing-the-system}{%
\subsubsection{2.1 Accessing the system}\label{accessing-the-system}}

The system is available at:
\href{https://gl-ecologie.odoo.com/odoo}{\textbf{https://gl-ecologie.odoo.com/odoo}}

Log in with the email address and password defined when your account was
created (either provided by your manager or specified by you). If you
have forgotten your password, click \emph{Reset password} on the login
page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

\hypertarget{navigating-the-main-menu}{%
\subsubsection{2.2 Navigating the main
menu}\label{navigating-the-main-menu}}

After logging in you will see the main application menu at the top of
the screen. The apps you will use most often are:

\begin{longtable}[]{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule\noalign{}
App & Used for \\
\midrule\noalign{}
\endhead
\bottomrule\noalign{}
\endlastfoot
\textbf{Employees} & Your employee profile \\
\textbf{Planning} & Shifts, availability, your schedule \\
\textbf{Project} & Projects, tasks \\
\textbf{Protocols} & Protocols, protocol visits and species \\
\textbf{Timesheets} & Timesheets tracking \\
\textbf{Approvals} & Approval requests status, validation and
tracking \\
\end{longtable}

Administrators also have access to the \textbf{Settings} app, where
system configuration is handled.

\begin{figure}
\centering
\includegraphics{gallery/homepage_no_admin.png}
\caption{Home page for non administrator users}
\end{figure}

\hypertarget{language}{%
\subsubsection{2.3 Language}\label{language}}

The system supports limited automatic translation of the platform to
multiple languages. Dutch and English are currently supported.

If you would like the platform language changed, please contact your
manager or platform administrator. If you are a manager/administrator,
you can chage the language for a specific user at:

\emph{Settings} → \emph{Users \& Companies} → \emph{Users} → (the
desired user) → \emph{Preferences} → \emph{Language}

\hypertarget{mobile-use}{%
\subsubsection{2.4 Mobile use}\label{mobile-use}}

The system works on mobile browsers. For the best experience when
checking your schedule or registering hours on the go, use the Planning
app in list or calendar view. The availability calendar works best on a
desktop.

In addition, Odoo has a mobile phone apps available for both
\href{https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.odoo.mobile}{\emph{Android}}
and \href{https://apps.apple.com/app/odoo/id1272543640}{\emph{iOS}}

\hypertarget{views}{%
\paragraph{Views}\label{views}}

The platform offers multiple types of ways to visualize information at a
given page, called views. The most common views are
\textbf{\emph{Form}}, \textbf{\emph{List}}, \textbf{\emph{Gantt}},
\textbf{\emph{Pivot}} and \textbf{\emph{Calendar}}. Which views are
available at a given time depend on the specific page. Furthermore,
different views will offer different features/operations.

You can pick which view you want by clicking on the respective icon
displayed at the top-right of the window:

buttons will appear on the top left section, underneath the menu bar
(often next to other Buttons.), a \textbf{cloud shaped button} for the

\hypertarget{your-employee-profile}{%
\subsubsection{3.2 Your Employee Profile}\label{your-employee-profile}}

Your employee profile \textbf{stores your personal preferences} that the
planning system uses when assigning shifts. \textbf{This profile can
only be edited by managers or system administrators.} If you want to
have your employee profile updated (for instance if your maximum number
of shifts per week changed, or you got access to a vehicle) please
notify your manager or system administrator and they will update your
profile.

You can find your profile at \textbf{\emph{Employees}} → (your name).

The following information is available:

\hypertarget{filling-in-your-availability}{%
\subsubsection{3.3 Filling In Your
Availability}\label{filling-in-your-availability}}

Before a manager can assign you to a shift, you must declare your
availability and have it \textbf{validated}. This is the most important
step in the planning workflow.

\hypertarget{how-availability-works}{%
\paragraph{How availability works}\label{how-availability-works}}

The system operates in \textbf{strict mode}: if there is no validated
availability entry for a given date and shift type, you are treated as
unavailable --- even if you are free that day. You must proactively
declare availability for every date and shift type you are willing to
work.

\hypertarget{step-by-step}{%
\paragraph{Step-by-step}\label{step-by-step}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Go to \textbf{Planning → Employee Availability → My Availability}
\item
  Two views are available: Calendar (default) or List:

  \begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
    \textbf{Calendar} view:

    \begin{enumerate}
    \def\labelenumii{\arabic{enumii}.}
    \tightlist
    \item
      Select on \textbf{one or multiple days} (control + click to select
      multiple non-consecutive days, shift + click to select all days in
      a specific range or simply click and drag across calendar days)
    \item
      Click on the \textbf{\emph{Add}} button that will appear on top of
      the Calendar, \textbf{next to the \emph{N selected}}
      \textbf{information box.}
    \item
      Fill in:

      \begin{itemize}
      \tightlist
      \item
        \textbf{Shift type}: the type of shift you are/are not available
        for (morning, evening, etc.)
      \item
        \textbf{Available}: check this box if you \emph{are} available;
        leave unchecked if you want to declare that you are \emph{not}
        available for that day/shift
      \end{itemize}
    \item
      Press \textbf{\emph{Add}}
    \end{enumerate}
  \item
    \textbf{List} view:

    \begin{enumerate}
    \def\labelenumii{\arabic{enumii}.}
    \tightlist
    \item
      Press the \textbf{\emph{New}} button at the top left of the
      screen.
    \item
      Fill in:

      \begin{itemize}
      \tightlist
      \item
        \textbf{Date}: the date you want to specify availability for.
      \item
        \textbf{Shift type}: the type of shift you are/are not available
        for (morning, evening, etc.)
      \item
        \textbf{Available}: check this box if you \textbf{\emph{are}}
        available; leave unchecked if you want to declare that you are
        \textbf{\emph{not}} available for that day/shift
      \item
        \textbf{Notes}: Any extra information the manager should know.
      \end{itemize}
    \item
      Press on the cloud shaped save button to save, or the cross button
      to discard.
    \end{enumerate}
  \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\begin{figure}
\centering
\includegraphics{gallery/employee_availability_add_entry_list_view_save_button.png}
\caption{New employee availability entry in list mode - Save button}
\end{figure}

\begin{quote}
\textbf{New availability entries are automatically sent for validation}.
\end{quote}

\begin{quote}
You need \textbf{one entry per date per shift type}. If you are
available for both a morning and an evening on the same day, create two
separate entries.
\end{quote}

\hypertarget{what-happens-next}{%
\paragraph{What happens next?}\label{what-happens-next}}

After you save, the entry status changes to \textbf{Validation
Requested} and your manager receives a to-do notification. Once the
manager validates it, you will receive a confirmation notification and

\hypertarget{inventory-tools}{%
\subsection{4. Inventory \& Tools}\label{inventory-tools}}

The inventory module is accessible via \textbf{Planning → Materials}.

\hypertarget{structure}{%
\subsubsection{Structure}\label{structure}}

Materials are organised in three levels:

\begin{verbatim}
Category  (e.g. Bat research)
  └─ Material type  (e.g. Bat detector SM4)
       └─ Material unit  (individual physical item, e.g. SM4 #003)
\end{verbatim}

Each \textbf{material unit} has: - A \textbf{status} (e.g.~In service,
Out for repair, Lost) - A serial number (optional) - A rental flag (if
the item is rented rather than owned) - A material type

\hypertarget{linking-materials-to-a-shift}{%
\subsubsection{Linking materials to a
shift}\label{linking-materials-to-a-shift}}

On a shift form, use the \textbf{Materials needed} field to attach one
or more material \emph{types} to the shift. This records which types of
equipment are required --- it does not automatically reserve individual
units or deduct stock.

\begin{quote}
Stock quantities (\texttt{booked\_quantity}, \texttt{needed\_stock}) are
manually maintained. There is no automatic deduction when a shift is
created.
\end{quote}

\hypertarget{checking-what-is-assigned}{%
\subsubsection{Checking what is
assigned}\label{checking-what-is-assigned}}

Open any material type to see the list of individual units and their
current statuses. Use the list view under \textbf{Planning → Materials →
Material Types} to get an overview of available vs.~booked quantities
across all types.

\textbf{Open shift} & A shift without an assigned employee --- available
for employees to request \\
\textbf{Shift type} & The time-of-day category of a shift (Morning,
Evening, etc.) \\
\textbf{Role} & The function performed on a shift (Field Worker, Project
Leader, etc.) \\
\textbf{Availability entry} & A declaration by an employee that they are
(or are not) available on a specific date for a specific shift type \\
\textbf{Validated availability} & An availability entry confirmed by a
manager --- required before an employee can be assigned \\
\textbf{Project} & A coordinated body of work (e.g.~a monitoring
contract) that contains tasks and shifts. Projects can be nested under a
parent (master) project. \\
\textbf{Task} & A discrete unit of work within a project, typically
corresponding to a specific protocol visit instance. Tasks group the
shifts assigned to that monitoring event. \\
\textbf{Location} & A named address entry linked to a project. Includes
a label, address, optional notes and tags, and a direct Google Maps
link. \\
\textbf{Protocol} & A standardised monitoring procedure defining how,
when, and for which species a field survey is conducted. Each protocol
has a date window, a type, and one or more protocol visits. \\
\textbf{Protocol visit} & A specific visit defined within a protocol,
including a date/time window, goal, weather restrictions, species
involved, and an optional dependency on a related (previous) visit. \\
\textbf{Species} & A plant or animal species tracked in the context of
one or more protocols. Each entry contains a name, optional scientific
name, description, tags, and a list of associated protocols. \\
\end{longtable}
